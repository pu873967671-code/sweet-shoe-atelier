## 1. Core loop
1. **Merge board** (Atelier workroom): tap a generator (costs 1 energy) → it pops a tier-1 material into a free adjacent cell.
   Drag two identical items together → one item of the next tier (merge-2). Board 7 cols × 9 rows (portrait) / same grid shown wider in landscape.
2. **Orders** (top strip of merge screen): 2–3 order cards from boutique customers, each needs 1–3 specific merged items
   (e.g. `Satin bolt (fab6)` + `Pearl strand (spk3)`). Deliver from the board → coins, XP stars, sometimes energy/gems/chest.
3. **Custom shoe commission** (existing designer screen): some story orders are "commissions": the customer wants a shoe with
   specific color/material/strap/heel/decor/charm. Required design parts must first be **unlocked by crafting** on the merge board
   (e.g. Glitter material needs `spk4 crystal` delivered once; Kitten heel needs `lea5 heel block`). Then design → pack → stars.
4. **Try-on runway** (NEW): after packing a commission, and from the Collection, show the finished pair *worn*: two girl's feet/lower legs
   in the shoes, framed from mid-calf down with both feet visible, 3 poses (stand, tiptoe, step), camera zoom, sparkles,
   customer reaction line, share-free "Save to Collection". Shoe art on the feet is the same layered dynamic renderer (color, material,
   pattern, strap, decor, charm, heel type) composited over leg art `leg_{flat|block|kitten|wedge}`.
5. **Boutique renovation** (meta): stars from orders are spent on renovation tasks in the shop scene (8 decor slots × 2 levels:
   rug, counter, shelf, sofa, mirror, flowers, chandelier, window). Each completed task: short celebration, unlocks next chapter
   content (new generator, new chain, new customers, new design parts) and raises boutique "charm" (bonus coins per order).
6. **Chapters**: Ch1 Little Workshop (fab + lea chains, 4 tasks), Ch2 Sparkle Street (spk chain, 6 tasks), Ch3 Ribbon Gala (rib chain, 6 tasks).
   Endless mode after Ch3 with daily orders.

## 2. Economy (tune with simulation in smoke test)
- Energy: max 100, regen 1 per 90 s (also while offline, capped), rewarded ad = +50 energy (CrazyGames rewarded), gems can refill.
- Generators have cooldown charge (e.g. 12 taps then 4-min recharge) — level 2 generator from renovation adds tier-2 drop chance.
- Item tiers 1–7 per chain; selling an item gives tiny coins (trash can = sell). Item info panel on tap: name, tier chain preview.
- Board spaces: start with ~45% cells covered by crates (ui_crate) that open when a merge happens adjacent; keep 20–25% free space target.
- Chests (ui_chest) drop occasional bonus items; bubbles (rare duplicate offered for gems/ad, pops after 60 s).
- Orders use unlocked chains only. First six new requests are single tier-2/3 materials. One permanent simple slot chooses an existing item or a real ≤2-merge result when available at issue time; otherwise prefer a chain whose remaining charges can supply the piece. Requests remain stable after issue and across saves; later selling/merging can change their reachability. See §31.
- Must be fully playable without ads; ads only as optional rewarded (energy, double coins, skip cooldown) and midgame between chapters.
